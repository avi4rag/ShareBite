## Summary
Let verified donors share fresh food through a three-step form and reuse saved surplus templates.
## Changes
Food details, uploads, allergens, pickup times, keyboard-editable map pin, private NGO invitations, required safety declaration and live safety estimates. Templates post with new times and explicit safe-handling confirmation.
## How to test
Sign in as donor, post food with a photo and a future deadline, then find it in discovery. Try an unsafe deadline and private NGO invitation. Save and repost a template.
## Screenshots / notes
Server remains authoritative for time, upload ownership and verification checks. Direct main commits authorized. Full browser coverage follows the core loop test.
